About Bristol Community Events

Bringing creativity and connection together through community workshops and events in Bristol.

Our Story

Bristol Community Events (BCE) was founded with the mission of connecting people through creativity. Our community-driven workshops — from pottery and candle making to crochet and drawing — are designed to inspire people of all ages to learn, share, and create together.

We believe art and craft are powerful tools for wellbeing and connection. Whether you’re joining us for your first workshop or returning to share your skills, BCE provides a welcoming space for everyone to explore their creativity.

Our Mission

  • 🌿 Foster creativity and sustainability through handmade workshops.
  • 💬 Build community connections across Bristol and beyond.
  • 🎨 Make learning accessible, fun, and inclusive for everyone.
